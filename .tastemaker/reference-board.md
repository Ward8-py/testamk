## Principles borrowed, not copied

- Let a single piece of real media dominate each viewport.
- Use generous typography and precise spacing instead of decorative layers.
- Make the next action unmistakable and consistent.
- Let horizontal overflow communicate that more services are available.
- Use real project photography as proof, with copy functioning as caption rather than explanation.

## AMK translation

- The service rail uses seven AMK photographs and opens a service-prefilled quote, rather than product detail pages.
- Editorial Project X/Y cards carry the proof beat without category badges or redundant descriptions.
- The blueprint close is retained because it is specific to building work and was selected by the user.
